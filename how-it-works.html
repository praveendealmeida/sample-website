How the daily strategy works

A simple, transparent approach to compounding daily returns.

The strategy

The platform follows a daily yield strategy: a portfolio of processing scripts works continuously, and returns are credited to your balance each day.

Because everything is tracked in real time, you can always see your current balance, the generation rate, and your upcoming payouts.

You stay in control — choose when and how much to withdraw using scheduled or instant payouts.
